Autobiography

Personal development

I was born on 3 July 2003 in Krasnodar. My parents encouraged my curiosity from early childhood: there were always many books at home, and I learned to read very early. It was my parents and close people who instilled in me a love of knowledge, perseverance and the habit of finishing what I start. The atmosphere in my family, where honesty, diligence and respect for people were valued, had a great influence on me. As a child, I was a very enthusiastic person: I was most attracted to drawing and foreign languages. I drew a lot and took part in children's creative competitions, which taught me to pay attention to details, to see beauty in small things and to work patiently towards a result. Like many children, I dreamed of various professions, but one aspiration remained unchanged — to do something useful and interesting that would benefit people.

Among the brightest memories of my childhood are my first victories in creative competitions, the first books I read in English and my first independent drawings, which I proudly showed to my parents. These small successes formed in me the confidence that hard work always brings results. My main hobbies at that time were drawing, reading, mathematics and learning English, which later played an important role in my professional choice.

From 2010 to 2021 I studied at Secondary School No. 71 in Krasnodar (school71krd.gosuslugi.ru). I graduated with a red certificate and a gold medal. The years spent at school became an important stage in my personal development. I actively participated in olympiads: I reached the regional level in Russian and English and took part in city intellectual games "What? Where? When?" between schools. These events taught me to think logically, work in a team and not be afraid of public speaking. I would like to express special gratitude to my teachers, who supported my interest in exact sciences and languages, helped me believe in my abilities and guided me in the right direction. I am proud of my school and its graduates who have achieved success in various fields, and I hope that one day I will be able to contribute to its history.

Professional development

The formation of my professional preferences began in childhood and at school. I always enjoyed solving problems that required logic and analysis, and my love of foreign languages opened access to a large amount of information. Parents and teachers played a great role in my career guidance: they helped me understand that mathematics and computer science are not just school subjects, but the basis for creating modern technologies and tools. It was at school that I realized that I wanted to connect my life with information technology and programming.

The choice of university was deliberate: in 2021 I entered Kuban State University at the Faculty of Computer Technologies and Applied Mathematics, programme "Applied Mathematics and Informatics". I chose this programme because it combines fundamental mathematical training with practical programming skills, which opens up broad opportunities for professional growth. During my bachelor's studies (2021–2025) I actively mastered disciplines in programming, databases, machine learning and mathematical modelling. The most useful for my professional development were courses related to algorithms, data analysis and software development.

A special role in my professional growth was played by the teachers of the Department of Information Technologies of KubSU, who not only passed on knowledge but also instilled an interest in research work, helped me understand complex topics and supported my undertakings. My bachelor's thesis — "Android application 'Foreign Language Self-Study Guide'" — combined my interests in languages and programming and became the first serious project in which I applied the knowledge gained in practice.

In 2025 I entered the master's programme at KubSU, programme 01.04.02 "Applied Mathematics and Informatics" (specialization "Programming technologies and development of information and communication systems"). My motivation was the desire to deepen my knowledge in machine learning and NLP, as well as to take part in scientific research. The theme of my master's dissertation is "Intelligent technologies for targeted personnel selection in the IT industry", scientific adviser — PhD in Engineering, Assoc. Prof. Poletaikin Aleksei Nikolaevich. This topic was not chosen by chance: it combines machine learning, natural language processing and the practical task of interaction between universities and the labour market. As part of my dissertation work, I have already developed modules of a digital service for data parsing and assessment of educational potential, and also took part in the publication of a scientific article. These successes strengthened my confidence in the correctness of the chosen path.

My hobbies — drawing, fitness and foreign languages — also contribute to professional growth. Drawing develops attention to detail and helps in preparing diagrams and visualizations, fitness fosters discipline and endurance, and knowledge of languages opens access to international sources and allows participation in intercultural communication.

Goals, plans and vision of the future

In the coming years I have specific goals: to successfully defend my master's dissertation, continue research in the application of machine learning and NLP for recruitment and education, and develop the created digital service, bringing it to a level suitable for practical use by employers and universities. In the longer term, I see myself as a specialist in Data Science and machine learning, capable of solving complex analytical problems and creating intelligent systems that bring real benefit to society.

Professionally, I plan to continue working on scientific projects, publish research results in peer-reviewed journals and participate in conferences. Creatively, I want to develop skills in data visualization and design to make my projects not only useful but also user-friendly. Personally, I plan to continue self-education, learn new technologies and languages, maintain physical fitness and find time for creativity.

Possible ways to implement my plans include further study in a doctoral programme, work in an IT company or research centre, as well as the development of my own projects. I am considering various alternative scenarios: from working in a large corporation to participating in a startup or a research laboratory. Each of these paths is interesting in its own way and allows me to apply my knowledge and skills.

The general vision of the future of my profession and field of knowledge is connected with the further intellectualization of decision support systems and the development of natural language processing and machine learning methods. I am confident that the future belongs to intelligent technologies, and I want to be part of this process. The most preferable scenario for me is work in the field of Data Science and ML, where I can combine scientific interest with practical benefit, constantly develop and contribute to the development of the domestic IT industry.
